01

Forecasting Climate Signals

MSc dissertation, R

Compared Singular Spectrum Analysis with ARIMA on four long-running environmental series: atmospheric CO2, ENSO Niño 3.4, NASA GISTEMP temperature and NSIDC Arctic sea ice, supervised by Prof. Andrey Pepelyshev.

  • Built SSA models with a 60-month window using Rssa, and ARIMA models chosen with auto.arima on AIC, AICc and BIC.
  • Produced 24-month forecasts and compared them side by side with forecast, dplyr and ggplot2.
  • Documented the full workflow so it can be reproduced from the repository.
